Weather Conditions

The summer of 2012 in San Francisco was characterized by the typical Bay Area microclimate - cool, foggy mornings giving way to sunny afternoons. The moderate temperatures (averaging around 62-65°F) created perfect conditions for outdoor festivals and events.
