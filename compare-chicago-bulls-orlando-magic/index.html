Iconic Moments & Records

Chicago Bulls Iconic Moments

"The Last Shot" (1998)

Michael Jordan's game-winning shot in Game 6 of the 1998 Finals, securing the 6th championship and completing the second three-peat.

72-10 Record Season (1995-96)

Set NBA record for most wins in a season (broken by Warriors in 2016). Won championship with 87-13 overall record.

"The Flu Game" (1997)

Jordan scored 38 points in Game 5 of the 1997 Finals while battling severe illness, leading Bulls to crucial victory.

Derrick Rose MVP (2011)

At 22 years old, became youngest MVP in NBA history, leading Bulls to best record in league (62-20).

Orlando Magic Iconic Moments

Shaq Draft (1992)

Selected Shaquille O'Neal 1st overall, transforming franchise. Won Rookie of the Year and led team to playoffs in first season.

First Finals Appearance (1995)

Reached NBA Finals in just 6th season, fastest in NBA history. Defeated Jordan's Bulls in playoffs.

Penny & Shaq Duo (1993-96)

Dynamic duo of Anfernee Hardaway and Shaq created one of most exciting teams in NBA, reaching Finals together.

Paolo Banchero ROY (2023)

Banchero won Rookie of the Year, becoming third Magic player to win award (after Shaq and Mike Miller).
